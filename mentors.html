For UC Davis scientists

Mentor a year of real research.

One high school student, one year, one project you shape together. Our mentors are UC Davis scientists — mostly grad students, but undergrads, postdocs, and faculty mentor too. If you do research here, you qualify.

The commitment

Wednesdays, 3 to 5 pm, on campus, through the school year, plus a little prep. You see your student through start to finish: from picking a question in the fall to their poster at the spring Science Fair.

Why do it

It's real mentoring experience, the kind that belongs on a CV, in a fellowship application, or in a grant's broader-impacts section. It comes with a community of mentors from departments across campus. And the students go further than you'd expect: last year four of them presented at the international fair in Panama, and one took first place overall.

Before you start

Four steps, all done before you work with students: a background check (fingerprinting via LiveScan), California mandated-reporter training, a short program-safety training, and a signed code of conduct. The trainings are online, and we walk you through every step.
