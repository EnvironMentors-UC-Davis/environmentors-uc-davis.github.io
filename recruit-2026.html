One student’s story

Marissa, Pioneer class of 2026

Marissa Bautista joined as a junior who had never worked in a research lab. Her UC Davis mentors taught her to count individual algal cells on a lab instrument, and she spent a month sampling the Arboretum waterway — discovering that rainfall and water flow drive the algae blooms.

That poster earned her a scholarship to the international fair in Chicago. As a senior she studied bird diversity in the Sacramento River watershed with her mentor Marie Klein — and a poster in Panama City.

“This group of mentees and mentors, forming this community on campus, is super valuable for anyone.”

Marie Klein · Marissa’s mentor, UC Davis

How it works

  • Wednesdays 3–5 pm at UC Davis (PES Building, Room 2005), September–May.
  • Completely free — no cost to join, pizza included.
  • First session: Wednesday, September 23 — campus tour and bowling.
  • By winter you work 1-on-1 with your mentor and scheduling gets flexible — it works around sports. Track athletes have done this program.
  • All grades welcome.
